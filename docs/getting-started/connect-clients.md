# Connect clients

When you finish, your RustDesk clients use your server and you are signed in on them.

## 1. Copy the configuration

In the console, open **Client setup**. It shows the configuration string and its QR code.

## 2. Import it into each client

- **Desktop**: copy the configuration string. In RustDesk, open **Settings → Network** and
  choose **Import server config**.
- **Mobile**: in RustDesk, open **Settings → ID/Relay server** and scan the QR code.

## 3. Sign in

In RustDesk, open **Settings → Account**, choose **Login** and sign in with your deskhaven
username and password.

The device appears in the console under **Devices** within a few seconds.
